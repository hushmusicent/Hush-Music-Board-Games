Privacy Policy — Hush Music Board Games

Last updated: November 22, 2026 · Developer: Hush Music Entertainment LLC

Hush Music Board Games ("the App") is a collection of classic board games you can play against the computer, pass-and-play with friends, or online against other players. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect the username or email address you provide and your password (stored only in hashed form). You may also add a profile photo.

Gameplay information. We collect your game results, points, wins, leaderboard standing, friends list, friend requests, and room activity so the games, rankings, and multiplayer features work.

Chat and voice. If you use in-game chat, your messages are stored so other players in the room can see them. If you use voice chat during a live game, audio is transmitted in real time between players in that room; voice audio is not recorded or stored by us.

Device and usage information. We automatically collect limited technical information such as device type, operating system, and app version to keep the App working and to fix problems.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account and keep you signed in.
  • To run the games, match players, track points and leaderboards, and deliver chat and voice in live rooms.
  • To prevent cheating, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • To improve the App and fix bugs.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which process information on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, Realtime Database, and storage) — used for accounts, game data, friends, chat, and voice signaling. Google's privacy policy applies to its processing: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Play — used to distribute the App and to process in-app purchases, if any. Google Play's terms and privacy policy apply.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your account and gameplay data while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and its data at any time by contacting us at the address below; we will delete it within a reasonable time, except for information we must keep for security, legal, or record-keeping reasons.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections and hashed passwords, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy here and update the date above. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
